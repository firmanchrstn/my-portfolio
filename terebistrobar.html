01. Background

Context & Project Goals

Terre Bistro & Bar is a favorite culinary destination in the Jababeka area, Cikarang. They offer a unique blend of Western dishes (like premium Steaks) and Indonesian culinary heritage (such as Soto Betawi).

The main goal of this project was to translate the restaurant's physical cozy, romantic, and elegant atmosphere into a digital form. The website was designed to attract new customers, simplify menu access, and facilitate seamless table reservations (including VIP rooms).

02. Challenge

Overcoming Information Complexity

In the hospitality industry, potential customers require a vast amount of information before visiting: Is there a parking lot? Is it kids-friendly? Are there smoking areas or private rooms?

The challenge was: Presenting all this data, along with customer reviews and an extensive menu list, without making the website interface look like a cluttered brochure. The website had to maintain the sophisticated presentation of a luxury brand.

03. Innovation

Technical & Design Execution

To create a memorable and premium experience (Cinematic Luxury), I implemented several advanced frontend techniques:

  • Lenis Smooth Scroll: Provides a buttery-smooth page movement effect, eliminating the laggy feel of standard browser scrolling.
  • GSAP ScrollTrigger: Utilized to create parallax animations on background images and an engaging horizontal scroll storytelling effect.
  • Bento Grid System: All facility information is summarized into a uniform, neat, and highly responsive CSS Grid architecture across all devices.
  • Interactive Menu: The full-screen background dynamically changes to match the specific menu item currently being hovered over by the user.
