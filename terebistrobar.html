01. Background

Project Context

Terre Bistro & Bar is a premium restaurant in Cikarang needing an exclusive digital identity to boost table reservations and brand awareness.

02. Challenge

Core Problem

Presenting extensive menu information and complete facilities without making the website interface feel rigid, boring, or cluttered.

03. Innovation

Crafting a "Cinematic Luxury" experience using buttery-smooth scroll animations, full-screen navigation, and a modern Bento Grid layout.
