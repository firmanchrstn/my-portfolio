01. Background

Context & Background

This project aimed to create a strong, dynamic visual identity for the DOMIN8 campus group. The primary requirement was to establish a bold, assertive, and memorable presence within the academic environment.

As the visual designer, my challenge was to translate the concept of "Domination" into graphical assets that could be applied seamlessly across various media, including digital platforms (social media) and physical print (posters and banners).

02. Concept

Art Direction

After researching the target audience (university students), I decided to steer clear of rigid, traditional corporate designs. Instead, I employed the following approaches:

  • Grunge Typography: Utilizing rough, textured typography to convey a rebellious and highly energetic feel.
  • High-Contrast Colors: Pairing deep monochromatic tones with sharp accent colors to immediately grab the viewer's attention.
  • Layering Elements: Incorporating digital scrapbook-style texture overlays to add depth and dimension to the poster.
03. Execution

Delivery & Output

All assets were designed cohesively. Utilizing tools such as Canva and Photoshop allowed me to arrange the composition and visual hierarchy with high precision.

The final output is not just a single static poster, but a comprehensive set of visual guidelines that the team can adapt for future campaigns, ensuring DOMIN8's brand identity remains consistent and impactful across all publications.
