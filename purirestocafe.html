Front-End Web Development

A Cozy Digital Home for Puri

A responsive landing page giving Puri Resto & Cafe a warm, earthy online identity with an accessible digital menu and a direct WhatsApp reservation flow.

01. Background

Context

Building a digital identity for Puri Resto & Cafe, a cozy culinary spot in Central Cikarang, to make it more accessible to customers.

02. Challenge

Problem Statement

The restaurant lacked an official website, making it difficult for potential customers to view the menu, explore the ambiance, and make online reservations.

03. Innovation

The Solution

A responsive landing page featuring an earthy design, digital menu integration, and direct booking access via WhatsApp.
