01. Background

Context & Background

Puri Resto & Cafe is a favorite culinary destination in Central Cikarang, offering rooftop vibes and live music. Although they have excellent offline reviews, they lacked an adequate digital presence beyond Google Maps.

This project was initiated to provide a professional online identity to help them reach a broader audience.

02. Challenge

Problem Statement

The absence of an official website made it difficult for potential customers to:

  • Discover popular menu catalogs and their prices.
  • Visualize the restaurant's ambiance before visiting.
  • Make practical table reservations without manually searching for contact numbers.
03. Innovation

The Solution

The proposed solution is a Landing Page Website developed using HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript. The key features of this website include:

  • Digital Menu Box: A Call-to-Action (CTA) integration leading to vmenu.id so visitors can easily view the updated digital menu.
  • Scroll Reveal Animation: Providing a premium user experience (UX) as the user scrolls through the page.
  • Direct WhatsApp Booking: A reservation button that directly links to the restaurant admin's WhatsApp, streamlining the booking process.
