01. Background

Context & Background

This project was created as part of a visual communication initiative aimed at transforming the campus environment into a more inspiring and emotionally engaging space for students.

The challenge was to design educational and motivational posters that felt visually personal, relatable, and capable of connecting emotionally with university audiences.

02. Concept

Creative Direction

The visual approach combined scrapbook aesthetics, mood-board composition, and relaxed typography to create an authentic and expressive design language.

  • Scrapbook Style: Layered elements and collage visuals to create emotional storytelling.
  • Relaxed Color Palette: Soft and balanced colors to maintain visual comfort and readability.
  • Visual Hierarchy: Carefully arranged typography and composition to guide user attention naturally.
03. Execution

Delivery & Output

The posters were designed digitally using Canva, focusing on consistency, readability, and emotional engagement through visual storytelling techniques.

The final output successfully combines educational messaging with modern visual aesthetics, creating a campaign that feels both approachable and visually memorable.
