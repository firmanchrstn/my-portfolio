01. Background

Context & Background

This project was designed as part of a social awareness campaign intended to encourage empathy, kindness, and positive interaction within the university environment.

The objective was to transform a simple act of kindness into a visually compelling narrative capable of emotionally engaging viewers.

02. Concept

Creative Direction

The visual direction was heavily inspired by cinematic movie-poster aesthetics, combining emotional storytelling with dramatic composition techniques.

  • Cinematic Layout: Applying movie-poster inspired framing and visual hierarchy.
  • Emotional Storytelling: Highlighting meaningful social interaction through expressive visuals.
  • Layered Composition: Combining typography, imagery, and depth effects for stronger narrative impact.
03. Execution

Delivery & Output

The poster was designed digitally using Canva with a strong focus on cinematic presentation, emotional resonance, and visual clarity.

The final result successfully communicates a heroic and inspirational atmosphere while maintaining a modern and visually engaging campaign style.
