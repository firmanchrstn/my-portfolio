UI/UX & Flutter Mobile App

Bridging the Digital Gap for Travelers

A Flutter mobile concept that restructures tourism information around clear hierarchy and progressive disclosure, so travelers can find what they need in fewer steps.

01. Background

Context

Redesigning a mobile application structure to bridge the digital gap for tourism users, making travel information easier to access and navigate.

02. Challenge

Problem Statement

Information overload and complex navigation patterns causing friction and cognitive overload when users look for tourism services.

03. Innovation

The Solution

A streamlined interface with clear visual hierarchy, progressive disclosure, and simplified navigation built as a Flutter application.
