01. Background

Context & Background

inDrive operates in one of the world's most competitive mobility markets, distinguished by its Real-Time Deals (RTD) or peer-to-peer fare negotiation model. This model disrupts algorithmic surge pricing by returning price control to humans.

However, despite its strong user growth globally, the actual execution of the interface creates unnecessary friction. The platform currently acts merely as a raw mirror of the database, transferring the burden of filtering information directly to the users. This project aims to redesign the platform into an intelligent assistant that eliminates information asymmetry while preserving the core philosophy of fair, human-driven pricing.

02. Challenge

Problem Statement

Based on extensive UX research and user review analysis, the current application suffers from four critical failure domains:

  • Accessibility Exclusion: The app fails WCAG 2.1 AA standards, rendering core interactions (like bidding and accepting orders) completely incompatible with screen readers like TalkBack, excluding users with visual impairments.
  • Unsafe Driver UI (Cognitive Overload): The driver dashboard mixes ride-hailing and courier orders in a single, chaotic queue. Drivers are forced to evaluate conflicting task types under time pressure while driving.
  • GPS Location Drift: High triangulation inaccuracies cause pickup pins to shift randomly, leading to mismatched pickup points, driver frustration, and an estimated 18-22% cancellation rate.
  • Negotiation Friction: Manual numeric fare entry creates guesswork, dragging the average bidding time to 87 seconds due to a lack of contextual price anchoring.
03. Innovation

The Solution & Architecture

The redesign strategy introduces a Next-Gen Platform architecture (PRD v1.1) focusing on Operational UX and intelligent backend integration:

  • Dynamic Bidding Slider & GenAI RAG: Replacing manual typing with an interactive color-coded slider. A Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) layer fetches real-time data (fuel price, weather, traffic) to provide fair price anchoring.
  • Modular Driver Dashboard: A tab-based navigation separating City Rides from Courier services. It includes a radius control slider and a Floating Compact Earnings Card to drastically reduce cognitive load.
  • Seamless Payment & Pre-Authorization: Integrating E-Wallet auto-deduct capabilities prior to bidding. A pre-authorization system protects drivers from unpaid trips while avoiding forced cash collections.
  • MIP Optimization Engine: Behind the scenes, a Mixed Integer Programming (MIP) engine balances customer wait time, driver distance, and fare deviation to recommend the best possible match instantly.

This comprehensive overhaul is projected to reduce cancellation rates, increase GMV, and provide a 100% WCAG-compliant experience.
