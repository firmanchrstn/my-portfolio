01. Background

Context & Background

Mobile JKN serves as the digital backbone of Indonesia’s National Health Insurance (BPJS Kesehatan), catering to over 200 million users.

This redesign project focused on transforming a bureaucratic healthcare platform into a cleaner, more accessible, and user-centered digital experience.

02. Challenge

Problem Statement

The application suffered from information overload, cluttered navigation, and weak visual hierarchy, making it difficult for users to quickly access essential healthcare information during urgent situations.

03. Innovation

The Solution

I implemented a card-based information structure, progressive disclosure system, clearer participant card hierarchy, and simplified service navigation to improve readability and usability.
