Overview

Project Overview

The rebirth of LinkAja was initially conceived as a competition project developed by our team, UXORA, through the Multimedia In Action (MIA) 2025 competition held by UPN ‘Veteran’ Jakarta. The competition commenced on 29 October 2025, allowing us to explore the world of UI/UX Design extensively.

The Problem

Problem Statements

Despite being a strategic collaboration between State-Owned Enterprises (BUMN), LinkAja faced major UX challenges, including low user satisfaction ratings, poor novelty perception based on UEQ research, cluttered and unintuitive interface design, and the lack of optimized essential features such as biometric login and simplified phone number management.

The Solution

Proposed Solution

To enhance both pragmatic and hedonic value, we introduced four key features: TapAja for seamless NFC-based payments, InvestasiAja to simplify access to digital micro-investments, ChatAja as an AI-powered assistant for instant issue resolution, and MainAja to improve user engagement through gamification and reward-based interactions.

Key Takeaways

Key Takeaways

This project highlighted the importance of balancing functionality with a "delightful" user experience. By simplifying the Information Architecture and introducing gamification, we aimed to turn a functional tool into an engaging financial ecosystem.

Design Focus

Disclaimer/Role Focus

As part of the collaborative UXORA project, my primary contribution during the prototyping phase focused on ChatAja, an AI-powered assistant designed to simplify access to financial services through natural language interaction. The feature provides instant 24/7 support, helps users resolve transaction and account-related issues efficiently, and delivers a clean chat-based interface aligned with the minimalist “LinkAja Reborn” design system.
