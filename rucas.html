rucas.co Project Overview

A conceptual UI/UX design exploration for Rucas, a premium local streetwear brand known for its exclusive, limited-drop collections.

The Challenge

The Challenge

High-traffic streetwear releases require a fast and intuitive user interface to manage high-demand product drops efficiently.

The Solution & UX Strategy

The Outcome

The result is a sleek, conversion-focused interface that bridges the gap between elegant UI/UX design and practical application architecture.

The Solution & UX Strategy

Brand Identity & Minimalism

I utilized a monochromatic color palette (black, white, and light grey) to emulate a high-end fashion editorial feel.

The Solution & UX Strategy

Frictionless Cart Interaction

Instead of forcing users to a separate cart page, I designed a Dimmed Overlay Cart Modal to reduce friction in the user journey.

The Solution & UX Strategy

Strategic Scarcity

I integrated clear "Sold Out" badges directly onto the product grid and added transparent purchase-limit warnings.
