The Challenge

The Challenge

High-traffic streetwear releases require a fast and intuitive user interface. The challenge was to design a 3-page e-commerce landing page (Home, About, and Products) that builds immediate trust, showcases high-quality apparel, and allows users to add items to their cart without being redirected away from the product catalog. Overview

Project Overview

A conceptual UI/UX design exploration for Rucas, a premium local streetwear brand known for its exclusive, limited-drop collections. The objective was to design a clean, modern, and highly scannable digital storefront that prioritizes brand storytelling and a frictionless purchasing flow.

The Challenge

The Challenge

High-traffic streetwear releases require a fast and intuitive user interface. The challenge was to design a 3-page e-commerce landing page (Home, About, and Products) that builds immediate trust, showcases high-quality apparel, and allows users to add items to their cart without being redirected away from the product catalog. The design needed to be visually striking yet highly structured for a seamless developer handoff.

The Outcome

The Outcome

The result is a sleek, conversion-focused interface that bridges the gap between elegant UI/UX design and practical application architecture. It delivers a premium shopping experience that matches the energy of the Rucas brand while maintaining strict usability standards.

The Solution & UX Strategy

Brand Identity & Minimalism

I utilized a monochromatic color palette (black, white, and light grey) to emulate a high-end fashion editorial feel. This heavy use of negative space ensures the user's focus is drawn entirely to the product photography and the bold, high-contrast typography.

The Solution & UX Strategy

Frictionless Cart Interaction

Instead of forcing users to a separate cart page, I designed a Dimmed Overlay Cart Modal. This allows users to review their order summary and adjust quantities while maintaining the context of the product page in the background, significantly reducing friction in the user journey.

The Solution & UX Strategy

Strategic Scarcity

Acknowledging the brand's business model, I integrated clear "Sold Out" badges directly onto the product grid and added transparent purchase-limit warnings inside the cart to manage user expectations during high-demand drops.

Key Features

Hero Section

A high-impact visual introduction that communicates the brand's exclusive vibe within the first 5 seconds.

Key Features

Bento-Grid Product Layout

A clean, symmetrical grid system that enhances scannability and adapts well to modern responsive web frameworks.

Key Features

Component-Driven Design

Buttons, typography styles, and spacing rules were designed with front-end implementation in mind, ensuring the UI is ready to be translated into clean code.
